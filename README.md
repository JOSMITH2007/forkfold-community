## Why Forkfold?

Most recipe apps lock your personal recipes behind cloud accounts and €30–50/year subscriptions. **Forkfold** is built around data ownership: your recipes live locally on your device, work 100% offline, and can sync directly as plain `.md` files to your local folder (Obsidian, Syncthing, Nextcloud).

## Core Features

- **100% Offline-First & Local Markdown Sync:** Sync recipes as clean `.md` files with YAML frontmatter to any local folder, or export print-ready A4 PDFs and JSON backups.
- **Smart Recipe Import:** Import from cooking websites (JSON-LD/OpenGraph), photos, plain text, or TikTok/Instagram/YouTube reels — offline, with your own Gemini API key (BYOK), or optional AI assistance.
- **Anti-Food-Waste & Seasonality:** Built-in seasonality scoring, pantry & best-before (MHD) tracking, live pantry matching on recipe cards, and meal-prep shelf-life ratings.
- **Tastefinder & Hands-Free Cooking Mode:** Swipe through your recipes to decide what to cook, navigate cooking steps using your phone's **hardware volume buttons**, and start auto-detected step timers.
- **Modular Focus Mode:** Starts clean and minimal. Enable optional power modules only when you need them: on-device OCR receipt scanning, price tracking, and serverless QR-code WG/flatshare expense splitting.
